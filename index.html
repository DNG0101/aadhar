📋 Tips for best results

    
1

      
Use a clear, flat photo — Aadhaar card on a dark background, QR fully visible, no glare or shadow over the QR

    

    
2

      
Higher resolution = better — Aadhaar Secure QR is very dense (177×177 cells). Scanner tries 8 different zoom levels automatically

    

    
3

      
Try the e-Aadhaar PDF screenshot — screenshot from mAadhaar app or UIDAI portal gives the best image quality

    

    
4

      
Algorithm: QR scan → decimal string → BigInt → bytes → pako.inflate (zlib) → split by 0xFF → ISO-8859-1 strings → 16 fields
