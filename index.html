💡 Tips for best results

    
1
Camera uses native BarcodeDetector + ImageCapture for hardware-accelerated scanning with sharper frames. Falls back to jsQR with contrast enhancement.

    
2
Mobile & Email are shown automatically from the QR — actual values if present (old XML format), or linked/not-linked status for Secure QR (hash only).

    
3
Old XML QR (pre-2018) contains the actual mobile number and email. Secure QR (post-2018) stores only SHA-256 hashes for privacy.

    
4
Advanced verification — tap the verify panel to cross-check your mobile/email against the stored hash using all known UIDAI hash strategies.
