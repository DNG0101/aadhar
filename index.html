💡 Tips for best results

    
1
Use e-Aadhaar screenshot — screenshot from mAadhaar app or UIDAI portal gives the sharpest QR image

    
2
Dense QR code — Aadhaar Secure QR is 177×177 cells. Scanner tries 10+ zoom levels automatically. Keep the full QR visible

    
3
Photo — stored as JPEG2000 (JP2) inside the QR. Decoded via embedded PDF engine. Click photo to enlarge

    
4
Mobile verification — SHA-256 applied N times (N = last digit of Aadhaar) then compared with stored hash per UIDAI spec
