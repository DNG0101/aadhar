💡 Tips for best results

    
1
Hold steady — camera blur is the #1 cause of failure. Tap to focus before scanning.

    
2
For uploaded images, higher resolution = better decode. Avoid screenshots of screenshots.

    
3
Secure QR codes are very dense. The entire black square must be within the frame.

    
4
e-Aadhaar PDFs: upload the PDF directly — the QR page is auto-extracted.
